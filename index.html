Privacy Policy for dx-breaker

Application: dx-breaker • Package: rzad.dxbreaker • Publisher: rzad • Effective Date: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to dx-breaker. This Privacy Policy outlines how our game handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy. dx-breaker is an arcade brick-breaking game and does not require account creation, registration, or submission of any personally identifiable personal information (such as your full name, email, or telephone number).

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. Local Device Storage: Gameplay progress, levels, audio preferences, and high scores are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store this information on external developer servers.

B. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob): The application displays banner and rewarded video ads via Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personally identifiable device data, such as:

  • Device Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device model, hardware specifications, and OS version
  • Ad interactions, impressions, and performance metrics
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location and localized ads)

3. Permissions Requested

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for ad delivery from Google AdMob servers.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify connectivity before ad loading.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides in-game haptic feedback during collisions.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates services provided by Google. You can view their privacy policies below:

  • Google Play Services & Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Data Disclosure

5. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You can reset or delete your Advertising Identifier anytime via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

6. Children's Privacy

dx-breaker does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe such data has been collected, please contact us immediately.

7. Contact

For any inquiries regarding this policy, please reach out to the developer at: rzad (contact email provided in Google Play Console store listing).
